02 · PROCESS SELECTION

Why A²/O + tertiary high-density sedimentation?

Process selection was based on influent biodegradability, nitrogen and phosphorus removal requirements, winter operation, footprint, energy use, operational reliability and engineering precedent.

SELECTED BIOLOGICAL PROCESS

Anaerobic–Anoxic–Oxic (A²/O)

A²/O was selected after comparison with oxidation ditch, SBR, UCT and five-stage Bardenpho alternatives. The final scheme prioritizes stable nitrogen and phosphorus removal, a relatively compact footprint, mature large-plant application and winter operability.
