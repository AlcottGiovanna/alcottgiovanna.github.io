PROJECT SNAPSHOT

A complete treatment-plant design project, not just a drawing set.

The project serves Yinchuan’s southern Xixia District and treats a mixed influent composed of 75% domestic wastewater and 25% pretreated industrial wastewater. The design integrates preliminary treatment, A²/O biological treatment, tertiary phosphorus polishing, disinfection, hydraulic profile design, and equipment selection.

    HYDRAULIC DESIGN
